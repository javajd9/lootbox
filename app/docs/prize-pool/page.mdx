# The prize pool

Prizes are paid for by $LOOT trading, not by ticket sales or deposits.

## Where the money comes from

pump.fun pays a share of every $LOOT trade's fees to the token's creator. LOOTBOX sends those **creator fees to a public treasury wallet**. Anyone can see every fee that arrives on Solscan. The address is on [Addresses](/docs/addresses).

The prize pool on the live page is the fees that have reached the treasury since the current drop opened, valued in US dollars when they arrived.

## Goals

Every drop has a goal: the cost of its bundle, including tax and shipping, at the prices locked when the drop opens. The first drop, the GTA VI Bundle, has a goal of **${RULES.firstDropGoalUsd.toLocaleString("en-US")}**.

The second the pool reaches its goal, [the draw](/docs/the-draw) starts. There's no wait and no manual step.

## How the money is spent

- Fees arrive in SOL. When a drop is won, the treasury converts what's needed to USDC on-chain, so every swap is a public transaction.
- The USDC goes to the purchasing account that pays for the order. That transfer is the **Paid** proof for the drop.
- The treasury's key never lives on LOOTBOX's web servers. A compromised website can't move treasury funds.

## What you can check

- Every fee coming into the treasury, on Solscan.
- Every payment out, and that it matches a drop's **Paid** proof.
- That the pool on the live page matches the treasury's history.
