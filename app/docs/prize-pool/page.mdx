# The prize pool

Prizes are paid for by $LOOT trading. There are no ticket sales and no deposits.

## Where the money comes from

pump.fun pays a share of every $LOOT trade's fees to the token's creator. The creator is LOOTBOX's **public treasury wallet**, so those **creator fees go to the treasury**, where anyone can see them arrive on Solscan. The address is on [Addresses](/docs/addresses).

pump.fun holds creator fees until they're collected. LOOTBOX collects them into the treasury once at least {RULES.collectMinSol} SOL is waiting, and whenever a full pool needs them to pay out.

The prize pool on the live page is the SOL in the treasury plus the creator fees still waiting to be collected, valued in US dollars at SOL's current price. Until a pool fills it's held as SOL, so it moves with SOL's price as well as with trading.

## Goals

Every drop has a goal: the cost of its bundle, including tax and shipping. The first drop, the GTA VI Bundle, has a goal of **${RULES.firstDropGoalUsd.toLocaleString("en-US")}**.

When the pool reaches its goal, the prize money is set aside and the ticket list is locked. [The draw](/docs/the-draw) follows {RULES.drawPauseMinutes} minutes later. Nobody has to press anything.

## How the money is spent

When a pool fills, before the draw, the treasury swaps the goal's worth of SOL to USDC on-chain and sends it to the purchasing account. The swap, the transfer and the drop's fingerprint are all in one public transaction, which is the drop's **Paid** proof. After that, a drop in SOL's price can't shrink the prize.

The order is charged to LOOTBOX's prepaid account with Zinc, the service that places it. The team tops that account up from the purchasing account. This part happens off Solana.

The treasury's key is held by LOOTBOX's drop engine on its servers. That's what lets the prize be paid the moment a pool fills. The key is never sent to anyone's browser. Every movement in or out of the treasury is public, but you are trusting LOOTBOX to keep the key safe. See [What you're trusting](/docs/trust).

## What you can check

- Every fee coming into the treasury, on Solscan.
- Every payment out, and that it matches a drop's **Paid** proof.
- That the pool on the live page matches the treasury's balance plus the fees waiting to be collected.
