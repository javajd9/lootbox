# Addresses

Every on-chain address LOOTBOX uses. All of them are published here before launch, and pinned on LOOTBOX's official X account so you can cross-check them in two places.

## LOOTBOX

| What | Address | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| **$LOOT token** | Published at launch | The mint on pump.fun matches this exactly |
| **Treasury** | Published before launch | Receives creator fees and pays for prizes |
| **Purchasing account** | Published before launch | Receives USDC from the treasury for each order (the Paid step) |
| **Proof publisher** | Published before launch | Posts snapshot, ticket-list and proof fingerprints |

## Excluded from every draw

| Wallet | Address |
|---|---|
| pump.fun bonding curve | Published at launch |
| Liquidity pool | Published at launch |
| Team and dev wallets | Published before launch |
| Treasury and purchasing account | As above |
