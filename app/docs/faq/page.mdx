# FAQ

### Do I need to sign up or enter anything?

No. Holding $LOOT is entering. Your tickets come from your balance automatically. See [Tickets](/docs/tickets).

### How many tickets do I have?

Every {RULES.tokensPerTicket.toLocaleString("en-US")} $LOOT of your average balance is one ticket. Look up your wallet in the **Your tickets** box on the [live page](/).

### Can I buy right before the draw to win?

It barely helps. Tickets come from your average across snapshots taken at random times, so a late buy only counts in the snapshots after it.

### Is the draw rigged?

It can't be steered. The ticket list is locked on Solana before the randomness exists, the randomness comes from ORAO VRF, and anyone can re-run the draw. See [How the winner is picked](/docs/the-draw).

### Can the team win?

Team, dev and treasury wallets are published and excluded. What we can and can't prove about this is on [What you're trusting](/docs/trust).

### When is the draw?

The second the prize pool reaches its goal, live on the homepage.

### I won. What now?

Go to [Claim](/claim) within {RULES.claimDays} days, sign a free message with the winning wallet, and enter your shipping details at a $0 checkout. See [Claiming a prize](/docs/claiming).

### Will you ask for my seed phrase or a payment?

Never. Claiming is a free message signature and a $0 checkout. If something asks you to approve a transaction, it isn't LOOTBOX.

### Who sees my address if I win?

Only the services that ship the prize, and LOOTBOX's server, which keeps it privately until the prize arrives and then deletes it. It's never published. See [Privacy](/docs/privacy).

### How do I know the prize was really bought?

The order is proven with a zkTLS proof of the order service's own response, and the payment is a public Solana transaction. See [The 8 proofs](/docs/proofs).

### Where does the prize money come from?

$LOOT creator fees from pump.fun, collected in a public treasury. See [The prize pool](/docs/prize-pool).
