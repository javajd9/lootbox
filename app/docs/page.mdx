# How LOOTBOX works

$LOOT trading fees fund real prizes. Holding $LOOT gets you tickets. When a prize pool fills, one ticket is drawn on-chain, the prize is bought and shipped automatically, and every step is proven publicly without revealing anything private about the winner.

These docs explain exactly how, and show you how to check it yourself.

## What makes it different

- **Nobody picks the winner.** The ticket list is locked on-chain before the randomness exists, and the randomness comes from ORAO VRF, not from us.
- **Nothing private is published.** The winner's name, address, email and phone are never stored by LOOTBOX or shown anywhere. Only a fingerprint the winner alone can check.
- **Steps are proven, not posted.** Screenshots can be faked. LOOTBOX publishes cryptographic proofs that come from the source (Solana, the order system, the tracking service, the winner's wallet).
- **You can check it all yourself.** The proof page re-runs every check in your own browser. You never have to take our word for it.
