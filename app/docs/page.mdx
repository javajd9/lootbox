# How LOOTBOX works

$LOOT trading fees pay for real prizes, and holding $LOOT gets you tickets. When a prize pool fills, one ticket is drawn on-chain, the prize is bought and shipped automatically, and each step gets a public proof. None of those proofs say who the winner is or where they live.

## Drop after drop

LOOTBOX is a queue of drops, not a one-off giveaway. The next drop opens the moment the last one is drawn, and anything a pool collected past its goal starts the next one. Keep holding $LOOT and you're in every drop. See [Drop after drop](/docs/drops).

## Fairness and privacy

The ticket list is locked on Solana before any randomness exists, and the randomness comes from ORAO VRF. LOOTBOX doesn't pick the winner and has no way to.

The winner's name, address, phone number and email are never published. What does get published is a fingerprint of the address that only the winner can check. LOOTBOX keeps the shipping address privately until the prize arrives, then deletes it.

Each step's evidence comes from where it happened: Solana, the order service and its carrier tracking, and the winner's own wallet. The proof page re-runs every check in your browser.

## Runs itself, in the open

Nobody at LOOTBOX picks a winner, places an order or posts a proof by hand. A program called the drop engine does every step, every minute, and publishes the evidence as it goes. See [How it runs](/docs/how-it-runs).

The code behind all of it, including these docs, is public on [GitHub](/docs/source). Each page ends with links to the files that do what it describes.
