# Claiming a prize

If your wallet holds the winning ticket, you have **{RULES.claimDays} days** to claim. Claiming is free, and you never pay anything or move any funds.

## Steps

1. **Go to [Claim](/claim), pick your drop from the list of drawn drops, and connect the winning wallet.**
2. **Sign a message.** This proves you own the wallet. It's a message, not a transaction: it costs nothing and can't move funds. Each message works once and expires after {RULES.claimSigningMinutes} minutes.
3. **Enter your shipping details at a $0 checkout.** It's a normal checkout page with a total of $0.00, so there's no card and no payment. Your details go straight to the order.
4. **Save your secret code.** It lets you confirm, privately, that the prize was shipped to your address (see [Privacy](/docs/privacy)). LOOTBOX deletes its copy when the drop is complete, so keep your own.
5. **When it arrives, sign once more** to confirm you received it. That signature is the drop's final proof.
