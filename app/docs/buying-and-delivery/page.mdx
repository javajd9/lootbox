# Buying & delivery

Once the winner has claimed, the prize is bought and shipped automatically. Nobody at LOOTBOX picks products, places orders by hand, or handles the winner's address.

## Buying

- **Products are locked before a drop opens.** Each item has a fixed retailer product ID (an Amazon ASIN) and a price cap, both shown on the drop. The order is checked against this list automatically.
- **The order is placed through Zinc**, a service that places retailer orders by API. It orders the exact locked products, shipped to the winner's address, and refuses to go above the cap.
- **It's paid from the treasury.** USDC moves on Solana from the treasury to the purchasing account. That transaction is the drop's **Paid** proof.

## Delivery

- Tracking numbers come back with the order and are registered with **AfterShip**, which follows the carrier until delivery.
- Each status change, from shipped to in transit to delivered, becomes a proof. See [the 8 proofs](/docs/proofs).
- **Tracking numbers are never published.** Carrier pages can show a delivery city or ZIP code, so proofs only include a keyed fingerprint of the number.
- A bundle can arrive in several packages. Each one is tracked.

## When it's done

When everything has arrived, the winner signs one last message with the winning wallet confirming they received it. "Delivered" is the carrier's word. The winner's signature is theirs.
