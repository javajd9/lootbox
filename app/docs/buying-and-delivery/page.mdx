# Buying & delivery

Once the winner has claimed, the prize is bought and shipped automatically. Nobody at LOOTBOX picks products, places the order by hand, or handles the winner's address.

## Buying

Each drop's products are locked before its pool fills. Each item has a fixed retailer product ID (an Amazon ASIN) and a price cap, both listed under **In the box** on the homepage and in the code, and the order is checked against that list automatically.

The order goes through Zinc, a service that places retailer orders by API. It orders exactly the locked products, ships them to the winner's address, and won't go over the cap.

The money comes from the treasury. When the pool filled, the prize's worth moved on Solana from the treasury to the purchasing account as USDC, and that transaction is the drop's **Paid** proof. The purchasing account then pays Zinc for the order in USDC, on Solana, at most the price cap plus Zinc's $1 order fee. Zinc sends back whatever the retailer didn't charge, or everything if the order fails. That payment is a public transaction too, and it's listed in the drop's **Ordered** proof.

## Delivery

Zinc follows the packages with the carrier, and LOOTBOX checks Zinc's record of the order every minute. Two points in that record become proofs: **Shipped**, when the first package starts moving (with the carrier and the time), and **Delivered**, once every package has arrived (with the time). Each is a zkTLS proof of Zinc's own record, with its fingerprint on Solana. See [the 8 proofs](/docs/proofs).

LOOTBOX never stores or publishes tracking numbers. Carrier pages can show a delivery city or ZIP code, so the proofs leave out tracking numbers, scan locations and the address.

A bundle can arrive in several packages, and it only counts as delivered once all of them have. If the carrier can't deliver a package, or nothing has arrived three weeks after the order, the team is alerted and sorts it out with Zinc.

<Rule rule="shipping" />

## When it's done

After everything has arrived, the winner signs one last message with the winning wallet at [getlootbox.fun/received](/received) to confirm they got it. "Delivered" is the carrier's word, and the signature is the winner's. It's published as the drop's last proof, with its fingerprint on Solana, and the drop is complete.

If the winner never signs, the drop completes 14 days after delivery without it, and the proof page shows the receipt as missing. Either way, once the drop is complete, LOOTBOX deletes its copy of the winner's address.
