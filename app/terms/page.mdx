## 2. Who can win

To win a prize you must:

## 3. How you take part

You take part by holding $LOOT in a Solana wallet you control during a drop. There's no sign-up and no separate entry fee.

- Every **{per} $LOOT** of your average balance during a drop is **one ticket**. Fractions round down, for each wallet on its own.
- Your average comes from snapshots of every $LOOT balance on Solana. {RULE_TEXT.snapshots}
- Only wallets that can sign a message count. Accounts run by a program (other liquidity pools, vaults, multisigs) and tokens held for you by an exchange are left out, because they can't claim.
- Your chance of winning is your tickets divided by all the tickets in the drop. It changes with every snapshot until the ticket list locks.

[Tickets](/docs/tickets) explains the math with examples.

## 4. The prize pool and the prizes

Each drop's prize pool is filled by the creator fees from $LOOT trades on pump.fun, collected into the treasury. When a drop's pool reaches its goal, the prize's worth is moved out of the treasury and the draw begins. {RULE_TEXT.leftoverFees}

## 5. The draw

When the pool fills, a final snapshot is taken, the ticket list is locked and its fingerprint is posted on Solana. The draw starts {RULES.drawPauseMinutes} minutes later. A random number from ORAO's verifiable randomness service picks one ticket, and the wallet holding it wins. Every step is published so anyone can check it: see [The draw](/docs/the-draw) and [Verify a drop](/docs/verify).

## 6. Claiming a prize

The winning wallet has **{RULES.claimDays} days** from the draw to claim at [Claim](/claim):

1. Sign one message with the winning wallet. It's free and can't move funds.
2. Enter a US shipping address at a $0 checkout.

{RULE_TEXT.unclaimed} The same happens if the winner isn't eligible (section 2) or can't give a US shipping address in time. A prize that's redrawn can't be claimed by the earlier winner.

LOOTBOX never messages you first, and never asks for your seed phrase or private key, for funds, or for a transaction to claim. Anything that does isn't LOOTBOX. You're responsible for keeping your wallet safe; a prize goes to whoever controls the winning wallet.

## 7. Shipping and delivery

{RULE_TEXT.shipping} The prize is bought from a retailer through Zinc and shipped by the retailer's carrier to the address given at the checkout. The winner can't change the address after the order is placed. Once the carrier marks the prize delivered, the risk of loss passes to the winner. If a delivery fails or a package is lost, the Operator works with the retailer and carrier to replace or reship it, but isn't responsible for carrier delays. Products carry their maker's and retailer's warranties only.

## 8. Taxes

The winner is responsible for any taxes on a prize. In the US a prize counts as income at its fair market value. Where the law requires it, the Operator will ask the winner for tax information (such as an IRS Form W-9) and may report the prize's value to tax authorities.

$LOOT is a token traded on pump.fun and other Solana markets. The Operator doesn't set its price or promise it will have any value. Holding $LOOT gives no ownership of LOOTBOX, no share of profits, and no claim on the treasury or any prize except by winning a drop under these rules. Token prices can fall to zero. ## 10. Privacy

A winner's name, address and phone go only to the checkout (Shopify), the order service (Zinc), the retailer and the carrier, to ship the prize. LOOTBOX keeps them privately until the drop is complete, then deletes them; only a salted fingerprint is published. [Privacy](/docs/privacy) has the details.

## 11. Fair play

The Operator may disqualify a wallet, and redraw from the same locked ticket list, if it finds tampering with a drop, exploiting a bug, or breaking these rules. If fraud, a technical failure or anything else outside the Operator's control stops a drop from running fairly, the Operator may pause it, redraw it with new randomness, or cancel it, and will say publicly what happened and why. The pool of a cancelled drop is kept for the next drop.
